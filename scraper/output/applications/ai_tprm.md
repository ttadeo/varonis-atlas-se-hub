# AI TPRM

- [](/_docs/)- Applications- AI TPRMOn this page# AI TPRM
Use the AI Third Party Risk Management (TPRM) application as part of your process for managing third-party risk. Because you use third-party vendors within the enterprise, AI risk can be introduced through the supply chain. The AI TPRM application augments your current TPRM processes with a focus on AI. You can review how AI is being used within products and services that you consume through your supply chain and take control of the risk introduced by third parties. Specifically, you can request that vendors provide you with an AI-BOM that you can analyze and review to ensure that AI used through the supply chain does not put you at risk.

The process starts on the Vendors menu item. Click **Add Vendor** to initiate a workflow for managing AI validation of a new vendor. Once you start the process, an Asana project is created and you are invited to it. You go through a workflow to supply information about the vendor and the project, or upload documents from which the system extracts the required information autonomously. When you are done, the system generates an assessment and initiates a process to which the vendor is invited. The vendor provides all required information, including the AI-BOM. When all information has been provided, the system validates it and provides a risk score and a final determination.

## Dashboard[​](#dashboard)
The dashboard gives you a top-level view of review processes being conducted with vendors that have AI within their offerings and thus can affect your AI risk posture. You can view the state of their review process, the types of AI technologies you are using through their products and services, a risk matrix, and more. The data widgets include:

- The total number of vendors being reviewed with a breakdown by category of the type of AI introduced through the third party. Vendor statistics also categorize the number of vendors by stage of the review process.
- A risk matrix with the x-axis representing exposure risk level and the y-axis representing consequence risk level. In each cell, the number of vendors mapped to those two dimensions is noted.
- Critical remediation issues required. For more details, navigate to the Issues tab.
- Number of issues by severity.
- Assessment progress for active assessments, broken down by evaluation topic within the validation workflow (y-axis) and the stage (Reviewed/Under Review/In Remediation). This helps you identify bottlenecks in the process and track outstanding work items.
- The number of vendors managed within the AI-TPRM process for the last 90 days.
- List of AI technologies discovered by analyzing vendor AI BOMs, along with the number of vendors using each technology. This gives you visibility into which technologies are being adopted through your supply chain and the approval status of each.

## Vendors[​](#vendors)
Create new vendor AI assessments and manage ongoing assessments and workflows in the Vendors tab. Each process can be integrated within your overall TPRM process while the step within the system is focused on the AI BOM and the AI resources that your vendors use. View the AI inventory of the vendor if they provide an AI-BOM.

## Issues[​](#issues)
Manage vendor AI issues as they arise within the assessment workflows.

## Report[​](#report)
Use the AI TPRM report to search through all AI TPRM issues, present or past.

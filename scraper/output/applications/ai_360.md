# AI 360

- [](/_docs/)- Applications- AI 360On this page# AI 360
Use the AI 360 application to gain full visibility into all types of risks present in your organization due to AI adoption, with data on usage, issues, policies, and safe adoption practices.

## Dashboard[​](#dashboard)
The AI 360 dashboard provides a single view of all types of risks and issues associated with both the AI you consume and the AI you develop. It offers key metrics on AI adoption, threats, and attacks, mapping your environment to best practices and frameworks such as MITRE ATLAS and OWASP. The dashboard covers both security and compliance issues, offering a complete risk snapshot and trend analysis.

The dashboard view updates based on the selection from the hierarchy menu. For example, if you select the All Organizations level, the first item in the dashboard, AI Safety Score, displays the safety score for the entire organization, computed across all organizations and projects. If you select a specific project, the content reflects only that project. If you choose an organization, the content reflects all projects that belong to that organization.

Here's a breakdown of each section to help interpret key insights:

- AI Safety Score: Displays the current safety score, offering a quick gauge of the overall posture of AI assets, with a trend line to monitor changes over the last three quarters.
- AI Inventory: Summarizes the total number of AI resources, highlighting shadow AI and unprotected assets to provide an overview of inventory security. For each item, the current number is displayed along with the change from the previous quarter. The AI inventory numbers are derived from the AI Inventory applications and will not show data unless you have linked your cloud accounts, code repositories, or AI services.
- Vulnerable AI Resources: Provides categorization of vulnerable resources based on severity level and helps you prioritize remediation. Severity is either configured by you (e.g., in pentests) or follows standard scoring such as CVSS.
- AI Compliance Overview: Shows compliance and security posture status across various frameworks, with progress percentages to facilitate tracking regulatory adherence.
- Issues by Severity: Displays issues by severity over time, making it easy to spot trends in emerging risks and your team’s threat resolution efforts. Issues are created by all modules of the system -- for example, a newly discovered unsanctioned model may create an issue, as may a vulnerability, a blocked prompt, or a compliance gap.
- Risk Category Treemap: Visual representation of risk categories by urgency to help prioritize areas needing immediate attention.
- AI Risk Insights: Provides actionable intelligence on observed AI threats, indicating which areas deserve your focus to strengthen your security posture. Note that only insights in the "AI Inventory" category can be clicked to navigate to the appropriate technology list.
- Research Feed: Displays recent research articles and news on AI security, keeping users informed about the latest AI TRiSM developments.
- Top 5 MITRE ATLAS and OWASP Risks: Lists the most frequent risks aligned with the MITRE ATLAS and OWASP frameworks. Click the toggle to switch between frameworks.
- Riskiest AI &amp; ML Resources: Lists up to 20 AI resources with the largest number of open issues, with an indication of how many issues exist by severity (critical, high, medium, and low).
- GenAI App Activity: Daily GenAI request activity for the past 90 days, with the values for the most recent day shown on the right.
- AI Resources in Inventory: A chart showing the number of AI resources by type discovered over the past 90 days.
- Open Audit Findings: Number of audit findings over time.
- Opened and Resolved Incidents: Number of incidents over time by type (open/closed/external). Click the legend icons to filter incidents by type.

## Report[​](#report)
This tab provides a report of all issues created or updated and allows you to search, modify filters, and produce a downloadable report. The tab uses an embedded AWS OpenSearch instance. [Click here for more information and a guide on how to use OpenSearch dashboards and reporting](https://opensearch.org/docs/latest/dashboards/).

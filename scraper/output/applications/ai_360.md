# AI 360

- [](/_docs/)- Applications- AI 360Export PDFOn this page# AI 360
AI 360 is your Applications-level command center for AI risk and posture. It aggregates signals from across the platform — inventory, security posture, compliance, runtime, third-party risk, investigation, and incidents — into a single place where you can review your overall AI risk picture, triage individual risks and alerts, create incidents, and export periodic safety reports.

AI 360 does not discover inventory, configure posture policies, run audits, manage vendors, or own the full incident lifecycle. Those workflows live on the dedicated product pages linked throughout this page; AI 360 is where those signals come together for review and action.

You access AI 360 from the global navigation menu under **Applications &gt; AI 360**. The global menu lists two subitems: **Dashboard** and **Report**. Once inside AI 360, the in-page top menu exposes four tabs: **Dashboard**, **Risks**, **Alerts**, and **Report**.

## Permissions[​](#permissions)
AI 360 is available to users with the default platform entitlement tier. Within that tier, the **Dashboard** tab and the **Report** tab are each gated by a dedicated AI 360 permission. The **Risks** and **Alerts** tabs currently use the same permission as the Dashboard, so any user who can see the Dashboard can also see Risks and Alerts.

If you cannot see a tab you expect, ask your administrator to confirm your entitlement tier and your AI 360 permissions.

## Dashboard[​](#dashboard)
The Dashboard tab opens on a page titled **AI Risk** and provides a single, scope-aware view of risk and posture across the AI you consume and the AI you build. It pulls in security and compliance signals, maps them to industry frameworks such as MITRE ATLAS and OWASP, and surfaces both point-in-time snapshots and trend data.

Use the hierarchy selector at the top of the page to scope the dashboard. You can select:

- **Tenant** scope, to see aggregated metrics across every organization and project.
- A specific **organization**, to see metrics rolled up across the projects in that organization.
- A specific **project**, to see metrics for just that project.

All widgets update based on the selected scope. Project-only widgets such as Lowest-Rated Projects are hidden when you scope to a single project.

The Dashboard contains the following widgets:

- **Summary** — Your current AI safety and risk score, with a trend line so you can monitor changes over recent quarters.
- **AI Inventory** — Total AI resources, with callouts for shadow AI and unprotected assets. Numbers come from [AI Inventory](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_inventory) and are populated once you have connected your cloud accounts, code repositories, or AI services.
- **Vulnerable AI Resources** — Vulnerable resources broken down by severity so you can prioritize remediation. Severity is either configured by you (for example, in pentests) or follows standard scoring such as CVSS.
- **AI Compliance** — Compliance and security posture across the frameworks you track in [AI Compliance](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_compliance), including progress signals from Compliance Compass.
- **AI Resources in Inventory** — A chart of the AI resources discovered, by type, over the past 90 days.
- **GenAI App Activity** — Daily GenAI request activity for the past 90 days, with the most recent day's values shown on the right.
- **AI Risk Insights** — Actionable insights surfaced from across the platform. See [AI Risk Insights](#ai-risk-insights) below for details.
- **Opened and Resolved Incidents** — Incident activity over time, broken down by status. Click a legend entry to filter the chart by that status. Incidents themselves live in [AI Incidents](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_incidents).
- **Lowest-Rated Projects** — When you are not scoped to a single project, this widget highlights the projects with the lowest safety scores so you know where to focus.
- **Issues by Severity** — Issues over time by severity, so you can spot trends in emerging risk and in your team's resolution efforts. Issues are created by every product area — a newly discovered unsanctioned model, a new vulnerability, a blocked prompt, or a compliance gap can all create issues.
- **Risk Category Treemap** — A visual breakdown of risk categories by urgency, so you can see at a glance where to spend remediation effort.
- **Top 20 Riskiest AI and ML Resources** — Up to 20 AI resources with the largest number of open issues, with a breakdown of issues by severity (critical, high, medium, low).
- **Top 5 MITRE ATLAS / OWASP AI Risks** — The most frequent risks mapped to MITRE ATLAS and OWASP. Use the toggle to switch frameworks.
- **Research Feed** — Recent research articles and news on AI security, so you stay current on AI TRiSM developments.
- **Open Audit Findings** — Number of open audit findings over time.

### AI Risk Insights[​](#ai-risk-insights)
The **AI Risk Insights** widget is generated by backend metric calculators that look across multiple product areas — inventory, posture, runtime, compliance, third-party risk, and incidents — so an insight can span more than one of those categories.

When the underlying calculators do not yet have anything to report, the widget renders a zero state rather than an empty card. As your platform fills with signals, insights populate automatically.

Insights are intended for orientation, not as their own workflow. Today, only the **Resources with Multiple Issues** insight drills directly into a filtered view in AI Inventory. Other insights tell you where to look next; you act on them from the corresponding product page.

## Risks[​](#risks)
The Risks tab shows the individual risk items that roll up into the dashboard's risk and posture metrics. Use it when you want to see, filter, and act on the underlying records — not just the aggregated counts.

The table lists each risk with columns for risk type, severity, source, affected entity, status, and the time the risk was last updated. You can sort the table and page through results.

To narrow what you see, use:

- The **Open / All** toggle, to show only open risks or every risk.
- The **search** box, to match on risk text and affected entity.
- The **risk type** filter, populated from the list of risk types currently in the system.
- The **severity** filter (critical, high, medium, low).
- The **status** filter (for example, open, in progress, ignored, resolved, archived).

Risk records come from the platform's source areas — [AI Inventory](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_inventory), AI Security Posture Management, [AI Compliance](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_compliance), [AI Third-Party Risk Management](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_tprm) (AI TPRM), [AI Runtime](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_gateway), and [AI Investigation](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_monitor) — so the **source** column tells you where each risk originated.

### Acting on a single risk[​](#acting-on-a-single-risk)
Click a row to open the risk detail drawer. The drawer has three tabs:

- **Info** — Identifying details for the risk, the affected entity, the source system, and a link out to the affected entity or source area so you can see the full context.
- **Remediate** — Shown only for risk types that have a built-in remediation flow. For these types, you can resolve the risk and, where required, leave a remediation comment that is recorded on the risk and on any incident you create from it.
- **Incidents** — Any incidents already linked to this risk, and a control to create a new incident from the risk.

From the drawer you can:

- **Create Incident** — Open a new incident in [AI Incidents](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_incidents) prefilled from this risk.
- **Ignore** — Mark the risk as ignored so it stops showing in the default Open view.
- **Reopen** — Move an ignored or resolved risk back to open.
- **Mark In Progress** — Indicate that someone is working on the risk.
- **Archive** — Remove the risk from the active table while keeping a record.

### Acting on multiple risks at once[​](#acting-on-multiple-risks-at-once)
Select rows in the table to enable bulk actions. From the bulk action bar you can **Remediate** (for risk types that support it), **Ignore**, **Reopen**, **Mark In Progress**, or **Archive** the selected risks in one step. Where remediation requires a comment, you are prompted for it once and the comment is applied to every selected risk.

## Alerts[​](#alerts)
The Alerts tab focuses on event-driven signals — things that happened at runtime, in your governance flow, or in connected sources — rather than the static posture findings shown under Risks.

The table lists each alert with the alert type, severity, source, affected resource, status, and timestamp. As with Risks, you can use:

- The **Open / All** toggle.
- The **search** box.
- The **alert type** filter.
- The **severity** filter.
- The **status** filter.

Alert sources include AI governance signals (use the filter labeled **AI Monitor** to scope to alerts from [AI Investigation](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_monitor)), runtime signals from [AI Runtime](/_docs/docs/applications/ai_gateway), and any other connected source that emits alert-shaped events.

### Acting on a single alert[​](#acting-on-a-single-alert)
Click a row to open the alert detail drawer. The drawer has three tabs:

- **Info** — Alert details, the affected resource, the source, and a link out to the resource so you can investigate further.
- **Remediate** — Shown only for alert types that have a built-in remediation flow.
- **Incidents** — Incidents already linked to this alert, and a control to create a new incident.

From the drawer you can **Create Incident**, **Ignore**, **Reopen**, **Mark In Progress**, or **Archive** the alert.

### Acting on multiple alerts at once[​](#acting-on-multiple-alerts-at-once)
Select rows in the table to enable the same bulk actions available for Risks: **Remediate** (where supported), **Ignore**, **Reopen**, **Mark In Progress**, and **Archive**.

### Alerts vs. Risks[​](#alerts-vs-risks)
Use **Alerts** for things that happened — a runtime event, a governance signal, a detection. Use **Risks** for posture and compliance findings that describe a state of the world (a vulnerability, a misconfiguration, a third-party finding, a compliance gap). Some sources produce both; the tabs are filtered to the appropriate shape.

## Report[​](#report)
The Report tab generates the **Safety Statistics Report**: a CSV export that captures your safety score and issue counts over a time window.

To generate a report:

- Select a **Project**.
- Select a **Date Range**.
- Click **Generate Report**.

The export is delivered as a CSV with one row per date in the selected range. Each row contains:

- The date.
- The safety score for that date.
- Issue counts broken down by **critical**, **high**, **medium**, and **low** severity.

Use the report for periodic reviews, trend reporting, or as input to your own dashboards.

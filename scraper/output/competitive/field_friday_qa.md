If they have an AI governance committee or IT apps team they most certainly will know how many AI systems they have deployed. If they don't know, start with 10. We presented a high level demo to a current SaaS customer earlier this week who is very early in the stages of their AI transformation. Just now forming an AI focused Data Secuirity team inlcuding business owners, etc. The tool showed as very comprehensi ve but also a bit overwhelming for them and ahead of their AI journey. Can you address how the tool can be leveraged to aid / expedite that process for them as they look to mature AI enterprise wide? Customer examples.. The product is designed to start with an easy entry point AI Inventory structured to guide them through the full AI lifecycle as they mature. Many customers will only be prepared to take this ﬁrst step in the journey and we can license accordingly. Eventually we will guide them through to SPM, Guardrails, Observability, and Compliance. Start small but have conﬁdence that you wont' need multiple solutions to achieve maturity in the future. How is this priced? What do we charge for shadow AI?

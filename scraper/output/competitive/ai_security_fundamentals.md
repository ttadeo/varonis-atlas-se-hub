Description
This course is designed to give you a strong industry-level foundation to have confident, credible AI security conversations in the field. Think of it as the groundwork we'll build on in future, more advanced training sessions.

Here's what to expect across 8 modules:
- What AI security is and why it's fundamentally different from traditional security
- The AI TRiSM framework and how Gartner maps the space
- How AI systems actually work (foundation models, RAG, agents)
- The AI attack surface — prompt injection, data leakage, jailbreaking, and more
- Why AI security is, at its core, a data security problem
- AI identities, agents, and non-human access risks
- The regulatory and compliance landscape (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001)
- Customer personas and how to navigate AI security conversations

Each module includes key terms, customer conversation guidance, and knowledge checks to lock in the concepts.

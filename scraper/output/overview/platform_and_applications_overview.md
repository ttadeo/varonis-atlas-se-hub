# Platform and Applications Overview

- [](/_docs/)- Overview- Platform and Applications Overview# Platform and Applications Overview
The TRiSM Hub is an AI security and GRC platform comprising a set of applications delivered as a SaaS for ensuring that you can onboard AI systems quickly and efficiently in a safe, secure, and compliant manner.

The platform provides various core services such as an AI events data lake, job scheduling (e.g., for performing pentests on LLMs and scans on models), account management services, and more. The platform also hosts various AI trust, risk, and security management (TRiSM) solutions, including an AI compliance tool, an AI Runtime Protection solution, AI security posture management, and more.

Applications can be licensed as needed and deployed onto the platform according to specific use cases and requirements. Together, these applications and services enable a safe and secure AI-adoption journey.

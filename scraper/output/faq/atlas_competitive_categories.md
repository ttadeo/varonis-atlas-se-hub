# The Three Competitive Categories Atlas Faces and How to Differentiate

## The Three Main Competitive Categories Atlas Faces

Atlas faces three main competitive categories: AI TRiSM Platforms, AI SPM Point Tools (including CSPM vendors), and Big Vendor Bundles. Here is how Atlas differentiates from each.

**Category 1: AI TRiSM Platforms** (Portal26, CalypsoAI)
These platforms cover parts of the AI security lifecycle but require significant integration work. Atlas differentiates by providing full AI lifecycle coverage — inventory, runtime protection, compliance, and TPRM — out of the box in a single integrated platform.

**Category 2: AI SPM Point Tools and CSPM Vendors** (Wiz, CrowdStrike, Orca, Tenable)
These are cloud-focused tools that discover and assess AI posture in cloud environments. They do not scan codebases, have no runtime protection gateway, do not automate compliance frameworks, and do not manage third-party AI risk. Atlas does all of these. AI SPM tools do cloud AI posture. Atlas does full AI lifecycle security — a fundamentally different scope.

**Category 3: Big Vendor Bundles** (Cisco/Robust Intelligence, Microsoft Agent365, Palo Alto/ProtectAI, SentinelOne)
Broad security platforms that add AI security as an extension to existing products. They have distribution advantages but are not purpose-built for AI security. Atlas is purpose-built from the ground up, providing deeper specialization across the full AI security lifecycle.

**Atlas's core differentiator across all three categories:** No other platform combines full AI lifecycle coverage (inventory → runtime protection → posture management → compliance → TPRM) with Varonis's data visibility depth. That combination is unique in the market.

## How Atlas Differentiates from AI TRiSM Platforms

AI TRiSM competitors like Portal26 and CalypsoAI cover parts of the AI security lifecycle — GenAI adoption management, shadow AI detection, inference layer security. They are not full lifecycle platforms. Atlas covers the complete lifecycle out-of-the-box without integration work: AI Inventory, AI Gateway runtime protection, AI SPM posture management, AI Compliance automation, and TPRM.

---

## How Atlas Differentiates from AI SPM and CSPM Tools

Wiz, CrowdStrike, Orca, and similar AI SPM tools are cloud-only. The specific gaps:
- No codebase scanning — they miss AI in GitHub/GitLab repos, Jupyter notebooks, Python packages
- No runtime protection — no AI Gateway to intercept, block, or modify prompts inline
- No compliance automation — no automated mapping to OWASP LLM Top 10, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act
- No TPRM — no third-party AI vendor risk management
- No LLM pen testing — no adversarial testing of LLM endpoints

Atlas provides all of these. Wiz does cloud AI posture. Atlas does full AI lifecycle security.

---

## How Atlas Differentiates from Big Vendor Bundles

Cisco/Robust Intelligence, Microsoft Agent365, Palo Alto/ProtectAI, and SentinelOne add AI security on top of existing broad security platforms. These are acquired or bolt-on capabilities — not purpose-built. Atlas was designed specifically for AI security from the ground up, with deeper specialization and tighter integration across inventory, runtime, posture, compliance, and TPRM. The key differentiating question: is AI security core to their platform, or a feature they added?
